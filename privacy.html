Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 17, 2026

Short version: our extensions work locally in your browser. We do not collect, sell or share your browsing data. The only information that ever leaves your device is your license key, sent to Lemon Squeezy to check that your Pro plan is valid.

Who we are

Quietkit is operated by Rus Sergiu, Republic of Moldova ("we", "us"). This policy covers this website and the following Chrome extensions: BatchPull, Unseen, Bookscout, Snaptype, Snapline, Specter and Storelens. Contact: risioja@gmail.com.

Data our extensions process

Extensions may access web page content, browsing history or files only to perform their single, stated purpose, and only on your device. We do not operate servers that receive this data, and we do not use analytics, advertising or tracking code inside our extensions.

  • BatchPull reads links and media addresses on the pages you choose and passes them to Chrome's download manager.
  • Unseen uses the history permission to remove visits to the sites you list. Your list of sites is stored locally (optionally as hashes). If you enable sync, Chrome Sync stores your settings in your Google account.
  • Bookscout reads book data from Amazon pages you open and may load related public product pages at a limited rate to show research data. Results are cached locally.
  • Snaptype watches the last few characters you type, only in memory, to detect your snippet shortcuts. Keystrokes are never stored or transmitted. It never runs in password or payment-card fields.
  • Snapline captures screenshots of the tab you choose. Images are stored only on your device.
  • Specter reads styles, colors, fonts and assets of the page you inspect, only while you use it.
  • Storelens reads public store and marketplace pages and public product endpoints of the stores you open or choose to track. Snapshots are stored locally.

Licensing and payments

Purchases are processed by Lemon Squeezy, our reseller and Merchant of Record. When you buy, Lemon Squeezy collects your name, email, billing country and payment details under its own privacy policy. We receive order information (such as your name, email and what you bought) to provide support and refunds. We never see your full card details.

When you activate a license, the extension sends your license key and a random installation identifier to Lemon Squeezy to verify it, and repeats this check about once a day.

Data stored on your device

Settings, rules, snippets, captures, caches and license status are kept in your browser's extension storage. Uninstalling an extension removes this data. You can also export or delete it from each extension's settings.

This website

This website does not use cookies, analytics or tracking. It loads fonts from Google Fonts, which may receive your IP address as part of delivering the font files. The site is hosted on a static hosting provider that may keep standard server logs.

Sharing and selling

We do not sell, rent or share personal data with third parties, except Lemon Squeezy for payments as described above, or when required by law.

Chrome Web Store Limited Use

The use of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Your rights

You may ask us what order data we hold about you and request correction or deletion by emailing us. Data stored only in your browser is fully under your control.

Children

Our products are not directed to children under 16.

Changes
