Refund Policy

Last updated: October 17, 2026

14-day money-back guarantee. If a Pro plan isn't right for you, ask for a refund within 14 days of purchase and you'll get your money back. No complicated questions.

Eligibility

  • First purchase of any plan (monthly, yearly or lifetime): full refund within 14 days.
  • Subscription renewals: refund of the latest renewal if requested within 7 days of the renewal date and Pro was not substantially used during that time.
  • Refunds are not available for licenses suspended for abuse or public key sharing.

How to request

Email risioja@gmail.com with your order number or the email used at checkout. We aim to process refunds within 3 business days. Refunds are issued by Lemon Squeezy, our Merchant of Record, to your original payment method; your bank may take 5–10 business days to show it.

After a refund

The license key is deactivated and the extension returns to its free plan. Your data stays on your device.

Cancelling a subscription

You can cancel anytime from the link in your receipt email or in your Lemon Squeezy account. Pro remains active until the end of the paid period, and you won't be charged again.
